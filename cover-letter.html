Dear Hiring Manager,

Re: Application for Early Childhood Educator / Childcare Opportunities

I am pleased to submit my application for an opportunity with your centre. I am currently completing my Early Childhood Education (ECE) diploma at Humber College, with expected program completion in May 2027. I also hold dual bachelor’s degrees in Science and Education, with Canadian equivalency verified by World Education Services (WES), and bring more than three years of full-time teaching experience across elementary and junior levels.

I currently work as an Early Childhood Educator with licensed childcare centres in Toronto, including Gold Circle Early Childhood Daycare Centre (part-time) and The Little Campus (on-call). These roles have strengthened my ability to adapt quickly to different classrooms and age groups, work collaboratively with educators, support daily routines, and contribute to safe, engaging, play-based learning experiences.

My approach centres on building warm, respectful relationships with children and supporting their cognitive, social, emotional, and physical development. I aim to create inclusive environments where children feel safe, confident, and encouraged to explore, communicate, and develop independence. I would bring a dependable, flexible, and collaborative approach to your team, along with a strong foundation in teaching and child-centred learning.

I hold Standard First Aid and CPR Level C certification and have a clear Vulnerable Sector Check (VSC), with medical and immunization records up to date. My Ontario College of Teachers (OCT) application is in progress. I would be grateful to be considered for suitable roles, including Early Childhood Educator, Childcare Assistant, Classroom Assistant, or Montessori Support Educator.

Availability: I am available for regular part-time work three days per week, Wednesday to Friday, until April 30, 2027. From May 2027 onward, I will be available for full-time opportunities. I have attached my resume for your review and would welcome the opportunity to discuss how my experience and enthusiasm could support your centre’s needs.

Thank you for your time and consideration. I look forward to the opportunity to speak with you.

Sincerely,

Keerthika Mohanmoorthy
