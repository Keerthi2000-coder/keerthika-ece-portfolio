🌱 Professional Biography

Teaching that starts with relationships

I am an educator with more than three years of teaching experience, working across diverse early childhood communities in the Greater Toronto Area. In licensed child care at The Little Campus and Gold Circle Early Childhood Daycare Centre, I have supported toddlers and preschoolers (ages 1–4), kindergarten children (JK–SK) and school-age children (Grades 1–5) through play-based, developmentally appropriate learning. Through Tutor Doctor and my own private in-home practice, I also work one-to-one with learners from preschool to secondary school, including public-, private- and home-schooled children and their families. Before coming to Canada, I spent three years teaching Science and Psychology and mentoring students in Grades 6–10 at a CBSE school in India.

I am completing my Early Childhood Education diploma at Humber College (expected May 2027), and my Ontario College of Teachers application is in progress. My goal is to begin a full-time role as an Early Childhood Educator in a licensed program, where I can create inclusive learning environments for children of all abilities, design arts-based and nature-inspired experiences, and continue to build strong partnerships with families and the community. Guided by my philosophy that teaching and learning is a kaleidoscope of possibilities, I will listen, observe and reflect alongside children so new possibilities can emerge.
